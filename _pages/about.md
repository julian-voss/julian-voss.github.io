I am a Research Fellow in the [Department of Political Institutions and Conflict](https://www.ips.mpg.de/3884/political-institutions-conflict) at the [Max Planck Institute for Political and Social Science](https://www.ips.mpg.de/3004/en) and a PhD Candidate at the [Institute for Social Sciences](https://www.uni-osnabrueck.de/fb1/sozialwissenschaften) at Osnabrück University.

My interests cover a range of topics in Comparative Politics. I study political attitudes and behavior in the context of contention, political violence, and autocratic politics. In my research I mostly rely on causal inference designs using observational data as well as survey experiments.

You can contact me at [julian.voss@ips.mpg.de](mailto:julian.voss@ips.mpg.de)
